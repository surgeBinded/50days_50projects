Handstand & planche

Simple home workout • no pull-up bar • tap a photo for full size

Weekly plan

Mon & Thu

Handstand, toe pulls, planche lean, wall/pike pressing, L-sit

Tue & Fri

Backpack rows, reverse snow angels, split squats, glute bridge, hollow hold

Wed & Sat

Light handstand balance and feet-to-hands pike slides

Sun

Rest or gentle mobility

Exercises

Chest-to-wall handstand

2 × 20–30 sec · Rest 60–90 sec

Walk feet up the wall; keep shoulders elevated and ribs tucked.

Wall toe pulls

3 × 3–5 · Rest 60 sec

From a stable belly-to-wall handstand, gently float toes from wall, then return.

Freestanding kick-ups

5–8 attempts · Rest 60 sec

Practise safe sideways exits; kick up smoothly from a lunge, not forcefully.

Planche lean

3 × 10–15 sec · Rest 120 sec

In a plank with straight elbows, push floor away and lean shoulders forward.

Wall handstand push-up

2 × 3–6 reps · Rest 2–3 min

With wall support, lower head with control and push back up. No weighted vest.

Feet-elevated pike push-up

2 × 5–8 reps · Rest 2–3 min

Feet elevated on stable chair; hips high, lower head forward and push up.

Push-up to pike slide

2 × 5–8 reps · Rest 60–90 sec

Keep hands fixed. Slide feet TOWARD hands while hips lift; return to plank.
